Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

Freedom AI (the “Service”) takes your personal information seriously and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information we collect and why, how long we keep it, and who we share it with. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. What we collect and why

WhenWhatPurpose
Signing in with GoogleName, email address, profile picture URL, Google account identifierIdentifying members, saving projects, inviting and showing teammates
Using the canvasDrawings, sticky notes, shapes, images and to-dos on your boards; project namesSaving boards and sharing them with teammates in real time
Contact formName, email address, messageReading and answering your inquiry
Using the ServiceIP address (for abuse prevention), page view countsPreventing misuse, statistics for improving the Service

We do not collect sensitive information such as national ID numbers or payment details.

2. How long we keep it

  • Member information and signed-in users’ boards: until the member asks for deletion or deletes the project
  • Boards created without signing in: deleted automatically 24 hours after the last edit
  • Team invite links: expire automatically 7 days after they are issued
  • Contact messages: the most recent 200 are kept; older ones are deleted automatically
  • Access records used for abuse prevention: deleted automatically within one minute

3. Processors and external services

We use the following external services to run Freedom AI. Each processes information only for the stated purpose.

  • Vercel — website hosting and data storage
  • Pusher — real-time synchronization between teammates
  • Google — account sign-in (Google Identity Services)
  • Render — AI background removal (images are sent only while being processed and are not stored)

4. Advertising and cookies

We show ads on the landing page and on the guide and blog pages. There are no ads on the workspace (the canvas).

  • Google AdSense: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • We also show Kakao AdFit and Coupang Partners ads, and may receive a commission through Coupang Partners.

We use browser storage (localStorage) to remember display settings such as your language. This information stays on your device.

5. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. Send a request through Contact us at the bottom of the home page and we will handle it without delay. You can also delete projects yourself inside the app.

6. Security measures

All communication is encrypted with HTTPS, and the server checks permissions so that private boards can only be opened by invited teammates. Administrator functions are protected by separate authentication.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page at least 7 days before the change takes effect.
