Freedom AI User Guide

Freedom AI is a web collaboration tool where several people can draw, place sticky notes and share to-dos on an endless canvas at the same time. This guide follows the order you will actually use the features, so a first-time visitor can set up a team board in about ten minutes.

1. Getting started: sign-in and your first project

There is nothing to install. Open Get started in an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox) on a PC, tablet or phone and the canvas opens right away.

  • Sign in with Google and your projects are saved to your account, so you can pick up where you left off on another device.
  • You can also look around without signing in. Boards made without signing in are deleted automatically 24 hours after the last edit, so sign in or save a PNG if you want to keep the work.

Open the project list in the top-left corner and choose New project to create an empty board. Click the board name and give it something recognizable, such as “Q3 marketing meeting”, so it is easy to find later.

2. Moving around the canvas

The canvas has no edges. When you run out of room, just move over and keep going.

Pick a tool from the toolbar or switch instantly with a keyboard shortcut. Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing text, so you can type freely.

4. Sticky notes and arrows

Sticky notes come in five colors. Giving each color a meaning makes a board much easier to read — for example yellow for ideas, pink for problems and green for decisions.

  • Double-click a note to write in it, and use the text-size button to change the font size.
  • Drag a corner to resize it.
  • Bring a note close to another and it snaps into line like a magnet.
  • Connect notes with arrows to show order or cause and effect.

5. Inviting teammates and real-time collaboration

Press the Invite button on a project to create an invite link. Send the link in any messenger; the person who receives it signs in with Google and joins the same board.

  • Invite links are valid for 7 days. Issue a new one when it expires.
  • Once you start inviting, the board becomes private and only invited teammates can open it. Any public link that was circulating before stops working.
  • People who joined appear in the invited members list, where you can also remove them.
  • Everyone’s cursor and name appear in real time, and whenever someone draws or moves a note it shows up on every screen immediately.

6. Calendar to-dos

Open the Calendar in the toolbar to see the to-do list attached to the project. Pick a date and write a task: every teammate sees the same list, and check marks update in real time. If you turn decisions into dated to-dos during the meeting, there is nothing left to tidy up afterwards.

7. Adding images and AI background removal

Drag a reference image file onto the browser window to add it to the canvas — handy for mood boards and collecting references.

Press and hold an image on the canvas and AI removes its background. It recognizes not only portraits but also logos and text images. While it works, the image is sent to the background-removal server, and only the result comes back to the canvas.

Tip — The clearer the color difference between subject and background, the cleaner the result. Blurry photos may get rough edges.

8. Saving and exporting

When you are signed in, your work is saved automatically. To paste a board into a document or a chat, choose Save as image (PNG) to download the current board as a picture.

9. Troubleshooting

  • A “sign in again” banner appears — This shows up when your sign-in session is about to expire. Click the banner once to extend it without choosing your account again.
  • I can’t see my teammates’ changes — Some company or school networks block real-time connections (WebSocket). Try a different network.
  • The invite link won’t open — Either 7 days have passed, or the board owner issued a new link, which invalidates the old one.
